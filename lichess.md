# Lichess

**Play:** [lichess.org](https://lichess.org) · iOS and Android · **Price:** Free · **Ads:** None

- Free and open source, run as a non-profit and funded by donations.
- Unlimited online games at every time control, with puzzles, studies and full computer analysis.
- Nothing is locked behind a subscription.

**Best for:** serious players who want a rating against real people and strong analysis tools.
**Less good for:** players who want a story or a campaign; it is plain, classic chess.
